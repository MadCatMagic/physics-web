The Bessel functions are a class of special functions that are solutions to a particular type of [[Ordinary Differential Equations|ODE]]:
$$
x^{2}\frac{ \partial^{2} y }{ \partial x^2 } +x\frac{ \partial y }{ \partial x } +(x^{2}-\alpha^{2})y=0
$$
where $\alpha$ is the **order** of the Bessel function. Solutions are denoted $\text{J}_{\alpha}(x)$. For example, the [[Slit diffraction#Circular aperture diffraction|diffraction of a circular aperture]] results in a first order Bessel function.

Sum to product formulae:
$$
\begin{align}
\sin\alpha+\sin\beta & =2\sin \frac{1}{2}(\alpha+\beta)\cos \frac{1}{2}(\alpha-\beta) \\[1ex]
\cos\alpha+\cos\beta & =2\cos \frac{1}{2}(\alpha+\beta)\cos \frac{1}{2}(\alpha-\beta) \\[1ex]
\cos\alpha-\cos\beta & =-2\sin \frac{1}{2}(\alpha+\beta)\sin \frac{1}{2}(\alpha-\beta)
\end{align}
$$

[[Linear Algebra]]

The determinant of a matrix is given in summation form as
$$
\det(A)=\epsilon_{i_{1},i_{2},\dots,i_{n}}a_{i_{1},1}a_{i_{2},2}\dots a_{i_{n},n}
$$
where we are summing over all $i_{j}$. It can also be found from the inverse formula as
$$
\det(A)=\sum_{j=1}^{n} (C_{F}^{T})_{ij}A_{ij}
$$
where $i$ can be chosen freely.

The #relativity/principle-of-relativity is that the laws of physics take the same form in any reference frame - it is impossible to determine absolute velocity, only velocity relative to some reference frame.

The classical transformation was the #relativity/galilean-boost which took the form
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
t' \\
x'
\end{pmatrix} =\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
v_{x} & 1
\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}
t \\
x
\end{pmatrix}
$$

#### Parabolic mirror
#optics/parabolic-mirror 
For a parabolic mirror, all parallel rays from infinity converge to $F$ *without any approximation!*. 

This is due to the property of a parabola that $|MF|-|MM'|=\text{constant}$, where $M$ is the point of incidences of the light ray, $F$ is the focal point, and $M'$ is the projection of $M$ onto the focal plane.
#### Spherical mirror
#optics/spherical-mirror 
Are easier to construct but introduce *spherical aberrations* #optics/spherical-aberration.
Both convex/concave mirrors form
$$
\frac{1}{u}+\frac{1}{v}=\frac{1}{f}
$$
but with $u$ and $v$ positive on the same side of the mirror, and with
$$
f=\frac{R}{2}
$$
where $R$ is positive for concave lenses, negative for convex lenses.
#### Newtonian telescope
#optics/newtonian-telescope 
Newtonian telescopes are constructed with mirrors, but they share the fact that
$$
M=\frac{f_{o}}{f_{e}}
$$
with regular [[Lenses#Refracting telescope|refracting telescopes]].
